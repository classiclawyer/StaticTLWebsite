# Brand artwork

The supplied archive is preserved byte-for-byte at `originals/Tamara-de-Launay-AI.zip`. Its Illustrator files are the original artwork; edit or convert copies only.

`variants/` contains web-ready SVG copies of all 21 supplied variants, organized as full logo, TL monogram, and name only. SVG paths and colors come from the PDF-compatible Illustrator artwork. Transparent variants are cropped around their artwork with a small margin; background variants retain their supplied background.

- Header and footer: `variants/full-logo/gold-transparent.svg`, on the existing purple backgrounds.
- Image watermarks: gold or purple transparent TL monogram, according to the existing background treatment.
- Favicon: `favicon.svg` uses the supplied purple transparent monogram, centered on a transparent square. PNG and ICO copies provide browser fallbacks. `favicon-256.png` is the high-resolution raster copy.

Do not recolor, redraw, or overwrite the source artwork when making future exports.
